# Free giveaways & sweepstakes — where to look

No bots, no auto-entry, no paying to play. Just the places that keep a live list
of legitimate free-to-enter giveaways, so you can skim and enter the ones you
actually want.

The rule that filters out ~95% of the junk: **a real sweepstakes never asks you
for money.** No entry fee, no "shipping fee", no "taxes up front", no gift
cards, no crypto. If money moves toward them, it's a scam.

---

## Set this up once (5 minutes)

1. **Make a throwaway email** just for entries — Gmail or Outlook, something
   like `yourname.sweeps@gmail.com`. Entering puts you on marketing lists;
   that's the actual price of admission. This keeps it out of your real inbox.
2. **Different password** than anything important. These sites get breached.
3. **Check that inbox weekly.** Most wins are announced by email and a lot of
   them expire in 48–72 hours. This is the single most common way people lose a
   prize they actually won.
4. **Never give** SSN, bank account, or a photo of your ID on an entry form.
   Name, email, age, sometimes a mailing address — that's all a real one needs.

## Instagram / social giveaways

You mentioned reposting ones — those are real, but it's the format scammers
imitate most, so check before you engage.

**Where to find real ones:** follow brands you'd actually buy from and local
businesses (much better odds — a local coffee shop giveaway might have 200
entries instead of 200,000). Also check the hashtags brands use for their own
promos rather than searching generic `#giveaway`, which is almost entirely spam.

**Signs it's real:**
- Verified or clearly established brand account with real follower activity
- Posted rules: eligibility, prize, start and end dates, how the winner is picked
- Comments from actual humans, not bot accounts

**Signs it's fake:**
- Account is new, few followers, or a near-copy of a real brand's handle
- Spelling and grammar errors in the post
- You're told you won something you never entered
- **Any** request for payment, a "shipping fee", a login, a password, a 2FA
  code, or a QR code scan
- Pressure to act immediately or lose the prize

**One hard rule:** if a DM says you won, don't click the link. Open Instagram
yourself, go to the brand's actual profile, and verify from there. Winner
notifications by DM from an account that isn't the sponsor are essentially
always a scam.

## Realistic expectations

People who win consistently enter a lot and treat it as a 10-minute daily habit,
not a lottery ticket. Prioritize:

- **Sweeps with many winners** over one giant jackpot — 10,000 product prizes
  beats a one-in-two-million car.
- **Daily-entry** ones, because entries compound.
- **Local and small-brand** giveaways, where the entry pool is a rounding error
  compared to a national promo.

You'll also owe taxes on anything substantial (US sponsors send a 1099 for
prizes over $600). Worth knowing before you win a trip you can't afford the tax
on.
